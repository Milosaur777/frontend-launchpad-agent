About Me

Hi, I'm Neo!

I'm Neo, a queer artist who works with traditional media like watercolour, graphite, oil, acrylics, ink, and printmaking. I enjoy creating cute, spooky and whimsical art, inspired by children's book illustrations.

You can sometimes find me selling in artist alleys at conventions; scroll down to see my con schedule, or follow me on @pigeon.f.arts on Instagram to keep yourself updated.
